Why “Respectful Resumes”?

“If you are respectful, you show consideration and regard for someone or something. Respectful is the adjective form of the word respect, which means a feeling or admiration.”
  • During the process you deserve and can expect respectful behavior. I will listen and give my assistance in a polite, professional manner.
  • At the end of the process, you should feel that your resume reflects your skills and accomplishments in a way that helps you stand out and will be admired.
  • Lastly, it is a little play on the saying “Respect Your Elders” and my last name, Elders.
